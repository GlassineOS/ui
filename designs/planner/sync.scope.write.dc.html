Writing into a repeating event: two choices, after the pause

Writing “room 3” after the typed title of this Thursday’s “team call”: the ink shows at once, and only when the pen pauses does a small card open under the title, “Change which events?”, with This event and All events (a text change has no “This and following”). The card never holds up the pen: the pen may go on writing in the field while it is open, and the answer applies to all the writing in that field until it is answered or the pen leaves the field. A tap outside the card changes this event only. Writing a new entry never asks.
