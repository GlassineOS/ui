More events at once than lanes: +N, and a fan

A lane is never narrower than about 160 px (about 4 cm on the panel), so that a word fits and the pen can land in it. When a group needs more lanes than fit at the width shown, the later events fold into a “+N” marker on the last lane, at the first folded event’s start; the lanes shown keep their brackets and titles. A tap on the marker fans them out over the group: the last lane’s event and the folded ones, as lanes when they fit at that width and as rows (time and title) when they don’t. The fan is for the moment: a tap outside closes it, and so does choosing one, which opens that event’s card. Opening and closing refresh only the group’s place. Here: five at once on the upright day agenda (three lanes and +2), and the same day in the small agenda widget at its preferred and its smallest size (one lane and +4). On this board you can tap the marker, an event in the fan, or outside it.
