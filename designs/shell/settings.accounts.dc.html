Accounts: calendars and contacts

The Accounts tab (shell spec 014). Each account is one row: its name, its status in the account service’s words (pim 001 FR-050), and the status’s action as a button on the row; a status that needs the person is set bold with a filled icon. A tap on the row opens the account. Below: Add an account, the calendar and address book on this device with their counts, Import a file. Without an account the list is one sentence. A status change redraws only its row; nothing moves. Settings itself connects to nothing: it shows what the account service says.
