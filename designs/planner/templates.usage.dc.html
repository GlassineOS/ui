Where a week template is used

The template list and the year’s list name a range of weeks by its week numbers and its months: weeks 1–13 · Jan–Mar, from week 14 on · from Apr, week 20 only. Each week counts in the month it starts in, as the year’s rows hold it; a choice that started in another year adds its year (from Apr 2027). Days and months are named by their dates and months.
