event.delete

Deleting an event: struck through, or held

There are three ways to delete an event, and all of them end in the same card. With the pen: one stroke across the title, from before its first letter to past its last. A stroke that starts inside the title, or doesn’t cross all of it, is writing. With a finger: held still on the title for 0.5 s. Or with Delete event on its card. The card opens under the title in one rectangle refresh: “Delete this event?”, the title as written, its time and day, Keep and Delete event. Keep, or a tap outside, closes it; the stroke goes and the event is as it was. Delete event removes the brackets and the title: only their place and the card refresh, and the time is free again. No system dialog. On this board you can strike the title with the mouse, hold it still, or tap it.
