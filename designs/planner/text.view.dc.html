text.view

Text mode for the whole view

From the cog: every handwriting field shows its text in a box; the agenda’s times and the mini month stay as they are. Ink with no reading (the arrow in the notes) stays visible and isn’t editable. The keyboard covers the lower part of the screen in one refresh; “Back to ink” is on its bar, far from the navigation. A field under the keyboard is reached by a finger tap after the page moves up once by whole rows (not shown).
