Home

Home is pages of widgets on a fixed grid: 4 columns × 5 rows in portrait. Apps come in three built-in widgets: Favourite apps (here), All apps and App category; each shows as many rows of four as it is tall and pages the rest itself. Every widget belongs to a page; adding one puts it on the last page, or a new one when that is full, so adding widgets never squeezes the others. The header pager (‹ 1/2 ›) turns pages. Tap an app to open it on a new screen; hold for its menu.
