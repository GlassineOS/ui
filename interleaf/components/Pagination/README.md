# Pagination

Previous and next page links at the foot of every page (Material's footer navigation).

- **Consumer provides:** two links with a label and the target page title.
- **White:** cards with a 1.5px `ink` keyline over `keycap`, `radius-card`.
- **Editorial:** square cards with a 1px `ink` frame and a hard `offset`; titles italic.
- **Stationery:** `card-stack` paper cards, `radius-paper`; titles italic.
- Cards wrap and stack on narrow screens; each is a single tap target of at least 44px.
