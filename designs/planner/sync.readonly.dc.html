sync.readonly

A read-only calendar: its card and its page

“Spring holiday” comes from Holidays, a read-only calendar. It shows like any all-day event, typed. Nothing the pen or a finger does changes it: a stroke over its title is not kept, a drag doesn’t move it, a strike-through doesn’t delete it; a line down the hour margin beside it makes a new event in the default calendar. A tap opens its card with the title, the day, “Read-only · Holidays” and only Open. Its page shows everything it holds and changes nothing: no − and +, no choices, no Delete event.
