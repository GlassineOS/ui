templates.range

Picking a range on the year

Two ways into a range. With the template first (Change on a range, or A range of weeks in the list), the lead says what to tap: the first and the last week for that template, then, after the first tap, only the last; the first is dotted beneath until the second tap saves the range. With the weeks first, a tap on a cell dots it beneath and the second tap opens the template list with the scope named after the range (Weeks 12 to 18), and the list asks for the template.
