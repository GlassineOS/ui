The planner’s settings: the calendars, a Show switch each

The planner’s settings (the cog) list every calendar of the store with a Show switch, all on at first: Personal on this device, Team and Family synced from cloud.example.org, and Holidays, read-only. Turning Team off takes its entries out of every view in one refresh; they are still synced and shown by other apps, and they are not counted or listed as hidden entries. A last row says that calendars are added in Settings and opens Settings on its accounts page; the planner has no account screens of its own.
