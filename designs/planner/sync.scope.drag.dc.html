Moving one occurrence: Change which events?

“team call” repeats every Thursday at 10:00. Dragged by its title to 11:00 and released, this Thursday’s occurrence sits dashed at its new time and a card opens under it, once, after the release: “Change which events?” with This event, This and following events and All events, each a whole line to tap. This event moves only this Thursday; This and following ends the series before it and starts a new one from it; All events moves every Thursday. A tap outside the card means no: the occurrence goes back to 10:00 and nothing changes. On the first occurrence of a series This and following would be the same as All events, so the card offers only This event and All events.
