Overview

Opened from the top bar’s screen button. Each screen is its live preview; tap it to go there. App icons sit in four slots around it, two per side, one per quarter; a window’s icon takes the first quarter it covers, so an app alone on its screen sits top left. A full-screen app’s icon has a heavy ring and its preview shows only that app (here KOReader on screen 3). Tap an icon to move, full-screen or close that app. Under each preview, icon buttons: move the screen earlier, close it with all its apps, move it later.
