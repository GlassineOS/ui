foundations.screens

Screens and windows

A screen holds one to four windows, one split each way. The word is “screen” everywhere (never workspace). Window management happens on app icons. The top bar’s screen button opens Overview, where apps and screens are managed; the dividers carry the layout. In Overview, icons sit in four slots around each live preview, one per quarter of the screen. Tapping an icon starts a move: switching pauses, icons appear inside the faded previews, and arrows beside an app place the moving app on that side. Close is always last.
