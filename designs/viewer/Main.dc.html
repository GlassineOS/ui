The board viewer, redrawn

The preview and the published designs today: a row of page buttons, a zoom slider, boards on grey, and a board view made of drop-downs. This design keeps the canvas, but the viewer becomes the place where a design proposal is reviewed: what changed, before and after, in every voice, and how the screens connect.

For whom

The user, looking at the boards by eye, and the reviewer of a design proposal. Agents keep shot and stream; the hub’s design view shows the same pages.

What was wrong

  • Boards draw their own state buttons, because the viewer has no place for states.
  • Zoomed out, labels and badges collide; there is no fit, no map, no search.
  • Nothing compares: no voices side by side, no before and after for a proposal.
  • The board view leaves most of the screen empty.

Rules for the viewer

  • Drawn in Interleaf White, paper or night; the boards in the voice the picker says.
  • A mark has a shape, never only a colour.
  • Static pages: no server, no accounts. “Seen” lives in the browser, comments on GitHub.
  • Every view is a link: board, state, voice, scheme, pen, compare mode.
  • Keys for everything: ⌘K J K ⏎ S N M 1 0.

The difference

“Changes” needs both revisions rendered and compared in the browser. The pixel boxes are cheap; naming them (“row is now the pair button”) is not, and is drawn here as if the proposal said it.

Flows

The arrows can be drawn from links and $states. A named flow and its walking order (“Pair a device”) is not in any file today: a list in canvas.json, or left out.

Real size

“Real size” and the device frame need the screen’s pixel density, which a browser does not know: a one-time measuring step, or only the frame without the size.
