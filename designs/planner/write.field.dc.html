write.field

Writing a to-do into the last free row

Raw ink (default state): the person’s writing as it is. Writing: a new to-do goes into the empty row while the list is full; the layout is held (marked here with a dashed box, which the planner doesn’t draw). Settled: after the pause, once, the list takes the new row and keeps an empty one; This week gives up the height. Neatened and Set in Autograph show the two opt-ins on every field: neatening straightens the ink towards the person’s own letters; Autograph sets each read word in the person’s font.
