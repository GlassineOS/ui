The change flow

Everything that changes the system goes plan → run → result, in the same three sheets: installing, removing, updating or restoring a package, removing an app of any origin, and installing at the end of setup. Only one change runs at a time; while it runs, the top bar shows it everywhere and every other change button is greyed with “Another change is running”.
