foundations.widgets

Every widget at every width

Each Settings widget in the four text widths a window can give it. Widths come from the window, not the device: the same widget meets all four on any page.

Window narrower than 560 pxTabs show icons only (56 px wide); Back and Refresh drop their labels; the page title drops from 26 to 19 px; side padding drops from 28 to 18 px.
Text width under 400 pxThe dithering picker drops from 4 previews in a row to 2.
Text width 380 px or lessSection notes on the right of a heading are hidden.
Text width 300 px or lessToggles drop their On/Off word; the switch alone shows the state.
HeightTabs that do not fit page through More · 1/2. Sections fold into rows, tallest first; lists never fold. What still does not fit pages with ‹ › at the bottom.
