Writing on paper

A pen tap into an empty text field lays a sheet of paper over the app, just below the cursor: milky and see-through, so whatever lies under it stays a faint, quiet backdrop and the ink always stands out. One dotted line to write on; keys and put-away in the corner. Write here to try it; a pause turns the ink into text (this board always reads “see you at 8”).
