Where a week template is used · two answers

How the template list and the year’s list name a range of weeks. A: by week number, as the bar names a week (week 10): weeks 1 to 13, from week 14 on, week 20 only. B: by months: January to March, from April on; exact only when a range starts and ends with a month, so a range in the middle of a month falls back to week numbers anyway. Days and months are named by their dates and months in both.
