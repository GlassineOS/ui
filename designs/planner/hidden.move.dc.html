hidden.move

Moving a hidden entry

Move in the hidden list: a sheet over the page with the entry in its ink, then the periods of its own kind to move it to. A day’s entry gets the month’s days (Monday first) with its day ringed; a week’s entry gets the weeks that start in the month, numbered with their dates; a month’s entry gets the year’s twelve months. The arrows step a month (a year for months). One tap moves it and goes back to the hidden list (closed when nothing of that kind is left hidden); the entry keeps its ink and its kind.
