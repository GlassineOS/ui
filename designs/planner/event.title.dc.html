A long title in a short event

A title written into a 30-minute block can’t run past it: its field grows down over the free time below (a dotted lower edge), never over another event, and the event keeps its time. When that space shrinks later (the event is shortened, another event takes the free time below, a smaller view, or the title set in the person’s font), the title shrinks evenly to fit, down to the smallest readable size. Below that it is cut at the block’s edge, with no “…”; a tap on a cut title shows the whole title, a tap on a title that is not cut opens its text mode.
