Overlapping events: shingled slips

Events that overlap lie as shingled slips. The later one lies on top, shifted 14 px to the right. The one underneath peeks out by a strip of at least 18 px that shows its title: its top while the one above starts later, its bottom once it is the one above. A tap on a strip brings that slip to the top; one refresh of the rectangle the slips share. Three or more overlapping slips lie the same way, and a tap on any strip fans them out side by side, at their own times, each a third as wide and turned 1.5° from the next, with no tab or folded corner: one refresh of the stack’s span. A tap on a fanned slip brings it to the top and closes the fan; a tap beside them closes it as it was.
