Neaten’s strength

How strongly Neaten straightens is the last word of its row, Light, Medium or Strong, underlined dotted as its own tap target: a tap gives the next one, Strong goes back to Light. The toggle beside it still turns Neaten on and off; the strength is kept while it is off. Every field that neatens redraws with the new strength at once; the ink as written never changes.
