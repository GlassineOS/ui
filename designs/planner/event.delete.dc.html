Deleting an event

Every timed event in a day agenda carries two grips in the soft ink: a short bar on its lower edge, where a drag changes its end, and six dots at its right edge. Held and dragged, the dots move the event as the block does anywhere. A tap on them opens the event’s card over the block (one rectangle refresh): the title as written, its time and day, and Delete event. The first tap on Delete event arms it; only that row refreshes, outlined, as “Tap again to delete”. The second tap deletes the event: the card closes and only the block’s place refreshes, free time again. A tap anywhere else closes the card, armed or not, and nothing is deleted. There is no undo and no dialog.
