Proposal: strokes between the brackets of a group

A proposal, not decided yet. Inside a group, a stroke can land outside every bracket. A stroke whose pen-down point is in the 12 px gap between one lane’s ] and the next lane’s [ goes to the nearest bracket, and its ink stays where it was written. A lane’s empty space, where its event has ended and the group goes on (right of the café, after the dentist), counts as free time: a line drawn down it makes a new event from the line’s start to its end, which joins the group in that lane, as a line down the hour margin does in an empty hour. What other ink written there belongs to is still open. Here: the café, the dentist and the call to the bank; “bring the card”, started in the gap left of the dentist’s [; a line down the dentist’s lane from 15:00 to 16:00; and the new event it made.
