home.add-widget

Add a widget

Built-in widgets (the three app widgets among them), widgets that apps bring, and your own scripts, in two columns on a sheet almost as wide as the screen: name, where it comes from, its sizes and when it refreshes, with an icon-only Add (a check once it is on Home). App category can be added any number of times and opens its settings right away. Add puts a widget after the last one and turns to its page.
