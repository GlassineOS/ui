event.stack

Events at the same time: one lane each

Every event is a pair of brackets, [ at the left and ] at the right; you write for it between them, as in a bracketed passage on paper. Events that overlap split the width into equal lanes for as long as any of them runs, the first start in the first lane, none narrower than about 160 px; a group that would need a narrower lane switches to one row each, its hours drawn taller (event.more). Each lane holds one event’s brackets, from its start to its end, its title at its start; neighbours meet as ] [, and the hour lines run on across. A stroke belongs to the lane its pen-down point is in, never to two events; ink outside every bracket is loose ink on the day. While the pen writes in a lane, the other brackets of its group turn light grey, and black again about a second after the pen lifts; a pen that reports hover shows this before it touches. Each event still moves, stretches, opens and is deleted as if it were alone. Here: the dentist and the call to the bank; working from the café around both; the pen writing in the bank’s lane.
