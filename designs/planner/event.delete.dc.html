event.delete

Deleting an event: crossed out or crumpled

Two ways delete a slip, and both end in the same card. The pen: one stroke through the slip that starts outside its left edge and ends past its right edge, or runs between opposite corners outside it; a stroke that starts inside the slip is writing. A finger held still on the slip for 0.5 s crumples it (one refresh of its place). When the pen or the finger lifts, a card opens below the slip (one rectangle refresh): its title, time and day, Keep and Delete event. Keep, or a tap outside, closes the card and the slip is as it was. Delete event removes it: only its place and the card refresh, free time again. No system dialog.
