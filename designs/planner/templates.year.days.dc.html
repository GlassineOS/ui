templates.year.days

The days of a year planned ahead

For day templates the year is a wall calendar: twelve months, three to a row, Monday first, a cell per day with its template’s letter and fill, today ringed and a single day’s own choice with the heavier edge. A range of days is picked the same way as weeks, its first day dotted beneath, and a range can run across months without leaving the screen. The arrows step a year.
