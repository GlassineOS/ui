Overview: moving an app

Tapping an icon starts a move. Switching screens pauses, previews fade, the screen actions hide, and every app’s icon appears inside its screen where its window sits; the moving app’s icon is greyed. Arrows beside an app put the moving app on that side of it, on any screen including its own (sides that would change nothing are left out; a 2 × 2 screen offers nothing). The moving app’s own actions form a half-circle around its greyed icon that points out of the screen: for a window narrower than the screen, Close app at the side edge with Full screen (Exit full screen when it already is) above and New screen below; for a full-width half, Close app at the top or bottom edge with Full screen left and New screen right. Where a spot is blocked or the preview is too short (landscape), that button takes the nearest free spot instead. Tapping another app’s icon picks that app instead; tapping the moving app’s own icon, the header’s ↶ or Escape cancels.
