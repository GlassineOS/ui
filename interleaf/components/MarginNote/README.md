# MarginNote

A short aside in the reader's hand, beside the paragraph it annotates. It is opt-in and part of the pen layer.

- **Consumer provides:** `[text]{ .gl-note }` after the paragraph (attr_list). Keep it under ten words.
- **Placement:** at 1200px and wider it sits in a `note-column` (180px) right of the text. Below that it drops inline under its paragraph and takes no extra width.
- **White:** Kalam (`note`) in `pen`, tilted −3°, often with ← pointing at the text.
- **Editorial:** italic Source Serif (`ed-note`) in `pen`, upright, may end with “— ed.”.
- **Stationery:** italic Garamond (`st-note`) in blue-black `pen`, led by a `gold` ❧.
- Don't: put facts only a note carries. Notes are optional reading.
