A long title beside a short event

A title written beside a 30-minute bracket stays in the event’s column: it wraps, and its field grows down over the free time below (a dotted lower edge), never over another title. The event keeps its time, and the bracket stays as long as the event. When that space shrinks later (the event is shortened, another event takes the free time below, a smaller view, or the title is set in the person’s font), the title shrinks evenly to fit, down to the smallest readable size. Below that it is cut at the column’s edge, with no “…”. A tap on a title opens the event’s card, which shows the whole title.
