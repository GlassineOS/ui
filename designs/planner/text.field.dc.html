text.field

Text mode on one field, and back

Ink (default): “call the bank about the card”, read as “call the bonk …”, and the planner shows only the ink. Text mode: a finger tap on the to-do; it becomes an ordinary text field and the system keyboard covers the lower part of the screen (one refresh of that area; nothing else moves). The bar above the keyboard holds this field’s own opt-ins and the way out. Back in ink: “bank”, the one changed word, is set in Autograph in its place; the other words keep their ink.
