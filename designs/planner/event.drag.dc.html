event.drag

Moving an event, and its ends

An event moves by its title. Pressed, its bracket turns dashed; dragged up or down, the dentist goes with the finger in 30-minute steps, and the margin’s readout shows its start. Each step redraws only the strip it leaves and the one it enters. On release the bracket turns solid again where it landed, in one refresh of that place. Each end of the bracket is pulled on its own: while held, its serif thickens, and the bracket follows it in 30-minute steps, with the readout at that end. Held past the first or last hour, the hours scroll a step per step and the readout stays at that edge; on release they go back, and an event outside them shows in the count at the edge. On this board you can drag a title or an end yourself.
