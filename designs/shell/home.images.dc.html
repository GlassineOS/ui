home.images

Images, before they are filled

A widget declares an image slot: a source and a size. Home lays the card out at once with a dashed frame and the slot’s alt text, then loads, scales, greys and dithers the image away from the widget’s render and fills each slot with one partial refresh of its own rectangle. Page 2 here, with its slots still waiting: a moon beside Tonight’s sky, today’s photo. On the Home board they fill a moment after it opens.
