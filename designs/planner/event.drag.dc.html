Dragging an event, and its edge

A finger holds the dentist’s block. The block looks as it will when released; the only mark of the drag is a dashed outline where it was. Each 30-minute step redraws only the strip the block leaves and enters (the dashed refresh marks are design marks; a few per cent of the panel, the spike counted about 3 %); the release redraws only the old place, as the outline goes. Its ink moves with it unchanged. Held past the agenda’s first or last hour, the hours scroll one step per step and the gutter’s readout stays pinned at that edge; on release they go back to the template’s hours and an event outside them shows in the count at the edge.
