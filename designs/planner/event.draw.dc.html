Drawing an event into free time

Drawing: the pen draws a line down the hour margin, over free time. It snaps to half hours, and the margin shows its start and end. Drawn: when the pen lifts, a new event exists from 16:00 to 17:00. Its bracket stands in the margin with its ends turned in, and beside it a dotted line marks where its title goes. Titled: the pen writes the title there; nothing is read aloud or shown as text. On this board a tap takes the next step.
