entry.open

An entry shown whole

One card for an entry the page can’t show in full: a title cut at its block’s edge (event.title), or an entry opened from the hidden list. The card lies over the entry’s place, as wide as it needs up to the page, and shows the ink as written, at its written size, wrapped where the writing wrapped; nothing in it can be edited. A tap anywhere closes it (one rectangle refresh); there is no Close button. From the hidden list it lies over the page a third of the way down, above the list.
