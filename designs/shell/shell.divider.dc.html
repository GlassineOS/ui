shell.divider

Divider

Tiling lives on the dividers. Tap a divider and its buttons appear on it: Swap, and on a two-window screen Direction (its glyph shows the arrangement it switches to; three or four windows already split both ways). No menu: apps and screens are managed in Overview from the top bar’s screen button. Tap a divider again, anywhere else, or press Escape to leave. Drag a divider to resize; only the line moves until release.
