Search results in the (i)

Search sits at the top of the (i) popover (answer B of info.search). Typing reads every entry of the planner and lists what matches as the keyboard is up: how many were found, then a row per entry in its own ink, cut to one row, with its period on the right (a day, a week number, a month). At most eight rows; past that the count says more than eight, and a longer word narrows them. A tap on a row opens that period with the entry in it. Nothing found says so in a line; the field stays as typed.
