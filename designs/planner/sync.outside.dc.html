An outside event in the day agenda, typed beside handwritten ones

An event the planner didn’t write (here “team call”, added on a phone to the Team calendar synced from cloud.example.org) shows like any event: its brackets at its hours, its title typed in the voice’s text face, straight on the line and in ink, where the planner’s own events are handwriting. Search finds it. Written into with the pen, it keeps its typed words typed and the new words are ink: “team call bring notes”, which the phone shows after the next sync. A cancelled outside event (“budget review”) keeps its place and lane, its title struck through with a light line. The all-day row holds the read-only “Spring holiday” typed beside the handwritten “bin day”. No calendar is marked by colour or pattern; only the card and the page name it. A sync that brings a change refreshes only the widget it is in, never while the pen writes.
