shell.keyboard.private

On-screen keyboard: private fields

Password and PIN fields, and any field the app marks as sensitive, are private: the strip shows a lock and says nothing is suggested or learned, the pen key is gone (no handwriting there), and nothing typed is kept anywhere. A PIN gets a plain keypad without letters. The strip keeps its place, so the keys don’t move when the field changes.
