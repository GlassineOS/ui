shell.keyboard.remove

Removing a suggestion

Hold a word in the strip (0.5 s) and the strip asks, in its own row, whether to remove it: no popup, one strip update. Removed, the word is never suggested again and isn't learned again; typing it still works. Here the keyboard once learned the typo “teh”: hold it, then Remove. The settings list every learned and removed word (settings.keyboard.words).
